Designs

Faceting designs drawn in Pleochroma, each with renders, its facet angles and the files to download.

1Published designs

Each design comes as a Pleochroma design file (.psdd), which keeps its material, size and crystal axis, and as GemCAD (.asc) and Gem Cut Studio (.gcs) files for other faceting programs, with its cutting instructions as a PDF in the standard sheet layout. The picture of a design shows it from above and from the side at the same scale; open a design for its renders, facet angles, proportions and files.
